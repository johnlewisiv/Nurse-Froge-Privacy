Study with care

Froge is a fictional nursing-study companion. The chatbot generates original NCLEX-style practice questions, explains answers and supports follow-up questions. It is not affiliated with NCSBN or the NCLEX examination. It does not guarantee an exam result or establish clinical competence.

AI can produce incorrect, incomplete or outdated questions, rationales and scoring. Check your current course materials and report suspected errors. Responses are not individually approved by a qualified nurse before you receive them. Froge is for education, not patient-care decisions, diagnosis, treatment, emergencies or employer policy. Use fictional cases; never enter patient details, confidential employer records, secrets or recalled live-examination questions.

Use an account and sign-in method you are authorized to use. Do not share or resell account access, automate bulk conversations, disrupt the service or attempt to access another person’s data. You keep your rights in your own text and allow the processing needed to provide the requested service, as explained in the privacy notice.

Froge’s life

Froge’s condition is a game mechanic, not a judgment about you. In the version 2 adult stage, the daily study deadline is midnight in your saved time zone, with a grace period until 4 a.m. Accepted substantive answers restore health without moving that deadline. Health falls over time; several questions can restore several health chunks, up to full health. A thoughtful wrong answer counts. Casual chat, opening the app and repeating an already accepted answer do not add care. Extra questions do not bank additional days.

The app pauses care while AI permission is withdrawn or a server-observed tutor outage is being resolved, and explains its current state. Using the free daily questions or reaching the end of a paid subscription does not pause the care clock. Time can pass while the app is closed; the service cannot detect every disconnected-device problem before the next interaction. A passed Froge stays in the story and a later generation begins. Payment never revives a dead Froge. Choose whether this dramatic style of accountability suits you.
